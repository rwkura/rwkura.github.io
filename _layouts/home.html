Sydney CBD Tuesday Run Club

Relaxed, social weekly runs around Sydney Harbour.

Sydney Harbour Runners are a friendly community of runners meeting every Tuesday night in Darling Harbour. There is a pace group for most runners, and new runners are always welcome! Just rock up, 100% free.

{% include icon.html name='map-pin' %}

Tuesdays from 6:00pm

Meet at the Cockle Bay Wharf Bird Fountain in Darling Harbour (Sydney CBD). Groups head off at 6:15pm.

{% include icon.html name='route' %}

Two Distances & Pacers

Choose Short (6-8km) or Long (11-13km) with volunteer pace groups from 4:30 to 6:15 min/km. Routes rotate weekly on our schedule.

{% include icon.html name='users' %}

100% Free & Social

No registration needed. Free bag drop is available, followed by post-run meals and drink specials with the crew.

Quick Questions

When & where do you meet?

Tuesdays at 6:00pm at the Cockle Bay Wharf Bird Fountain in Darling Harbour. Runs leave promptly at 6:15pm.

How do I join?

Just rock up! There is no need to register and we are 100% free to join.

Any questions?

Join the Sydney Harbour Runners Facebook group and make a post, everyone's happy to help!
