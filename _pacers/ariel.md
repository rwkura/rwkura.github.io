Ariel joined SHR at the start of 2024, shortly after moving to Sydney from Singapore. She has always enjoyed running and did track and field in school, but had never run anything more than 10K before moving to Sydney, as it was simply too hot back home!

Since joining SHR, she’s ticked off her first half marathon in 2024 followed by a full marathon in 2025, inspired by the people and running community she found at the club.
